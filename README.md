### 👨‍💻 Executive Summary for Recruiters & Engineering Leads

I am a **results-driven Full-Stack Engineer** specializing in architecting and deploying **high-concurrency web applications, multi-tenant enterprise SaaS products, and headless e-commerce storefronts**. 

* 🚀 **E-Commerce & Headless:** Engineered production headless storefronts using **Shopify Hydrogen**, GraphQL Storefront API, and custom server-side caching serving thousands of daily active shoppers across GCC and international markets.
* 🏢 **Multi-Tenant SaaS:** Designed robust scalable architectures powering salon management, billing systems, and inventory management with role-based access control (RBAC), multi-currency dynamic calculations, and dual-payment integrations (Stripe & Razorpay).
* ⚙️ **End-to-End Delivery:** From initial **Figma / Canva UI/UX design** to scalable **Node.js/PostgreSQL** backends and production deployment on **AWS, VPS, Coolify, Hostinger, Render, and Nginx**.
